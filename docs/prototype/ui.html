1 · Onboarding

One small window with four states. No passwords anywhere. Click into the email field in 1a, and type a name in 1c or 1d.

  1. Sign in and sign up are the same screen. The email decides: a new address gets one extra step (1c).
  2. One email with a magic link and a 6-digit code. The link opens quipcall://auth/… (we already handle quipcall://join). The code is for people who read their mail on a phone.
  3. Passkey: yes, but not as a button. A new user doesn't have one yet, so the app offers it once after the first sign-in (1c, Touch ID). After that, macOS suggests it as soon as you focus the email field (see 1a), and the key icon asks for it directly.
  4. GitHub and Google work now. Apple is greyed out with "soon", or hidden until it works. Your call.
  5. Guest means just a name. Opened from a call link, the screen shows the room and who's in it, with mic and camera toggles, so it doubles as the pre-join screen. Guests keep nothing, and the footer says what signing in adds.

2 · Home

Rooms and history on the left, the selected call on the right. Design review is live right now. Click the other rooms, the History tab and ‹ ›.

  1. Two panes. The list is on the left and the selected call on the right. Narrowed below ~600 px, the window turns into today's single list, and a click opens the call view over it.
  2. A room row has one avatar (whoever you talk to most there) with +N, the name, and the last call's date and length. Clicking a row opens the latest call. It doesn't join.
  3. Live rooms get a pulsing dot, the running time and a Join button. Other rooms show a call button on hover.
  4. History lists every call by day, quick calls included, with small marks for a transcript, a recording and files.
  5. Quick call stays at the top. + makes a room (with a real name field this time). Your account sits at the bottom.
  6. A live room's transcript can be read before you join. Late joiners get the history anyway (ARCHITECTURE.md).

3 · Finished call

The same window with a call that's over: a summary, the transcript as a chat, recordings and files. Press ▶, or click any line to play from there.

  1. The transcript is a chat. Others are on the left with avatars, you're on the right. Joins, screen shares and files appear in the timeline where they happened.
  2. The summary comes first (Stage 10), with action items. It folds away.
  3. The transcript follows the recording. When there is a recording, a player docks at the bottom. Click any line to play from there, and the line being spoken gets an outline. The screen-share marker in the timeline opens that recording.
  4. ‹ › steps through earlier calls in the same room.
  5. Recordings and files each get a tab with a count. Zero is fine, and the empty state says how to get some (open Family, or the solo quick call in History).
  6. A quick call gets Keep as room, for when a one-off call becomes a regular one.

4 · Call: basic

One window: tiles, a floating glass toolbar, and a side panel for the live transcript, files and people.

Layout Side panel
  1. The layout picks itself. Alone, you get a waiting card with the link. With 2–4 people it's a grid, with 5 or more a speaker view with a strip. When anyone shares, the share goes big and faces move to a column. Switch above to see each.
  2. Toolbar: mic ▴, camera ▴, share, record | panel, minimal | Leave. That's today's set plus record. Invite moved to the title bar and the People tab.
  3. The ▴ next to mic and camera picks devices. The noise filter switch lives there too: Strong (DeepFilterNet), Light (WebRTC), Off for music. That's the Stage 4 switch.
  4. Recording and transcribing show as pills in the title bar, so nobody is recorded without seeing it.
  5. Side panel: the live transcript (with who's transcribing and an off switch), files, and people with the invite link. Drop a file anywhere on the window to add it to the call.
  6. ⤡ switches to the capsule and closes the window, like today. Closing the window does the same. Only Leave ends the call.

5 · Call: minimal

Everyone in one floating capsule over your work. Hover it for the rest of the controls, and click the green timer in the menu bar.

Variant Controls
  1. One window holds all the faces. A glass capsule replaces both the window per person and the separate toolbar. Mic and Leave are always there. Camera, share, record, link and expand slide out on hover.
  2. Drag it anywhere. Dropped near the left or right edge, it turns vertical (Column).
  3. Scroll or pinch over the faces to resize them (36–96 px; Big faces is 84). The speaking ring, muted badge, name on hover and the mic button on your own face work as they do today.
  4. More than 6 people: a +N chip opens the rest, and the active speaker always gets a visible slot.
  5. Shares keep their own floating window because they need the room. Fullscreen works as it does today.
  6. Menu bar: the call's time sits in the menu bar. Its menu has the call controls, your rooms with call buttons, and Quick call. That's the start of Stage 4's menu-bar mode.
  7. Build cost: faces in one window lose the free compositor clip that each circle window gets today. There are two options: draw each face with round_video's bands (already exists, ~25 clipped strips per face), or teach our vendored GPUI to round a surface in its shader. I'd patch the shader: one quad per face and no extra CPU.

Questions for you

  1. Who can read a room's transcripts and recordings: everyone who was in that call, or every room member (including the live transcript before joining)?
  2. Where do recordings live: on the Mac of whoever pressed record (free, but only they have it until it's uploaded), or on the server through LiveKit egress (everyone gets it, but it costs VPS CPU and disk)?
  3. Text chat in a call: none (the transcript is the chat and files drop in), or a small message box under the transcript?
  4. Light mode: follow macOS, or stay dark only? Compare with the switch at the top.
  5. Accent: keep today's blue, or pick another swatch.
  6. Default call mode: the capsule (like today) or the basic window? Should a 1:1 call start differently from a group call?
