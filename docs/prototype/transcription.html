Transcription and recording · round 3

Your round 2 decisions are built in. Record saves the call's audio, or the audio plus your screen, which you pick as when sharing. There is one recording per call, and only whoever started it can stop it. You'll find it in the widget (sections 1 and 4), the call window (5), Settings (6) and Home after the call (7). Transcription works as before: one person's Mac transcribes everyone, and the transcript opens as a chat in a window of its own. Everything is clickable, and the conversation is made up.

1 · Live call

Hover Share, Mic or Leave: the row that opens has Transcribe and Record. Start either one, then hover or click the buttons that appear in the row, open ⋯ in the transcript window, or drag the window by its top.

Transcript Recording Model on your Mac Conversation
  1. Start: Transcribe joins Camera, Copy link and Call window in the hover row under the buttons. It opens a small panel that asks whose Mac does it (section 2). You're picked first when your Mac has a model.
  2. Record sits next to it and opens its own small panel: audio, or audio and your screen (section 4). The recording is made on your Mac, so there's no "whose Mac" choice. Pick "Audio and screen" to see the picker.
  3. While it runs, everyone's widget gets a captions button with a red dot, left of Share. Nobody is transcribed without seeing it. Click it to open or close the window. Hover it to see who transcribes, with which model. Share, Mic and Leave don't move, because the widget grows from its left end.
  4. While someone records, a red record button joins everyone's row too, left of the captions button. Hover it for what's recorded and the time, and Stop if it's yours. Other people also get a notice when a recording starts, and Record leaves their hover row: one recording per call.
  5. The window is a chat: others on the left, you on the right, and joins, leaves and handovers as dividers. The line being spoken shows in grey and fills in word by word (with a streaming model), then turns solid. Lines in Russian are there on purpose: the model detects each line's language.
  6. Closing the window stops nothing. The captions button brings it back. Stop for everyone, Copy transcript and Hand over live in ⋯.
  7. It's a floating panel like a share window: above other apps, on all Spaces, never takes focus. It opens beside the widget, and you can drag and resize it.
  8. Try the handover: set Transcript to Anna, then press "Anna leaves the call". The transcript pauses and everyone whose Mac can transcribe is asked to carry on. Try it with Recording set to Anna too: the recording stops, stays on Anna's Mac, and Record comes back for everyone.

2 · Who transcribes

Choosing whose Mac does the work, asking someone, and what happens when they leave. Frozen states.

  1. The list only shows people who can transcribe: desktops with a model. Web participants and Macs without a model are summed up in one line, so the list stays short in a 10-person call.
  2. Asking someone puts a prompt in their widget's notice row (the slim row under the main one that already shows "Joining…" and permission prompts). They accept, or say not now. In Settings, a Mac can be set to accept on its own.
  3. No model on your Mac yet: Download and start fetches the selected model right there and starts when it lands. Or ask someone who has one.
  4. When the transcriber leaves, the backend's lease runs out, the transcript pauses, and everyone who can is asked to take over. The first to accept wins (ARCHITECTURE.md, single transcriber).
  5. Handing over works the same way from ⋯ in the window. Your Mac keeps transcribing until the other person accepts, so no lines are lost in between.

3 · Transcript window

Its states, frozen. Everyone in the call sees the same transcript; only the line under the title changes.

  1. People who join late get the history (the backend stores it), then a divider marks where they came in.
  2. It scrolls itself while you're at the bottom. Scroll up to read, and new lines collect in a blue pill instead of pulling you down.
  3. Stopping saves it with the call. Home shows it in the call's Transcript tab, as the round-1 prototype drew it (prototype/ui.html, section 3).

4 · Recording

Record saves the call on your Mac: everyone's audio, or the audio plus your screen. Frozen states.

  1. Two buttons, two jobs. Transcribe makes text that everyone gets. Record makes a file on your Mac. Each starts and stops on its own. When both ran, Home plays the recording under the transcript (section 7).
  2. Audio is the default: everyone's voices mixed into one .m4a, about 30 MB an hour. It reads the same per-person audio the transcriber already reads, so it costs next to nothing.
  3. Audio and screen records your screen, not the call. You pick a display or a window in the share picker, with the same presets (5 or 15 fps for text, 30 or 60 for motion). Quipcall's own windows are left out, as when sharing. It's one .mp4, up to about 1.4 GB an hour.
  4. It costs about what sharing does: the same ScreenCaptureKit capture into IOSurfaces, the same VideoToolbox H.264, plus writing to disk. If you're already sharing that screen, the recording reuses the share's capture instead of starting a second one.
  5. One recording per call, and only whoever started it can stop it. While Anna records, Record is gone from everyone else's hover row, and the red button says whose Mac has it. If Anna leaves, the recording stops and stays on Anna's Mac, and anyone can start a new one.
  6. A crash loses almost nothing. The file is written in fragments, so a crash or a full disk keeps everything up to the last few seconds.

5 · Call window

In regular mode the toolbar gets the same two buttons, Record and Transcribe. The transcript gets no extra window here: it lives in the side panel's Transcript tab, with the call's timeline. Try the record button, the pills in the title bar, the tabs, or Stop.

  1. Two toolbar buttons, Record and Transcribe, between Share and the side panel. Record opens the same panel as in the widget. Transcribe starts the transcript (with the same chooser as the widget) and opens the Transcript tab. The pills in the title bar show what's running.
  2. People tab: hover someone whose Mac can transcribe and an Ask to transcribe button appears. That's the second way to assign the transcript to a person.
  3. Switching between the widget and this window keeps the transcript running; only the place it's shown changes.

6 · Settings

A new Settings window (⌘,), also opened by the gear next to your name on Home and by the links in the Transcribe and Record panels. On Transcription, change the language, download a model, select it, or try it with your voice. The Recording tab is new in round 2.

  1. Two settings, as you asked: Language and Model. The language changes which model is suggested and greys out the models that don't know it. Auto-detect is for mixed calls.
  2. Models download only when chosen and live in Application Support. Remove one with the button that appears when you hover it. The app bundle stays the same size.
  3. Try it runs the selected model on your mic, so you can hear the difference before a real call.
  4. The models: Parakeet TDT v3 (NVIDIA): 25 European languages, Russian included but not Chinese, very fast. Nemotron 3.5 ASR (NVIDIA, June 2026): streams word by word, 32 languages ready to use (40 locales in all), Chinese included. I couldn't confirm Russian. Qwen3-ASR 0.6B (Alibaba): 30 languages and 22 Chinese dialects, Russian included. The best fit for Chinese. Whisper Large v3 Turbo (OpenAI): 99 languages, but slower, and it waits for pauses. GigaAM v3 (Sber): Russian only, small, about 5–8% WER on FLEURS-ru. Apple Speech: built into macOS 26+, nothing to download, but the language list is up to macOS and Russian wasn't on it at launch.
  5. Recording sets what the Record panel picks first, whether each voice gets its own track, and the folder (Movies › Quipcall). The screen's frame rate is picked in the share picker, as when sharing.
  6. Sizes are rough (8-bit or 5-bit quantised), and the "behind your voice" times in Try are made up. The Stage 9 bake-off measures both.

7 · After the call

Home opens on the call you just left. Press ▶ or click any line to play from there, and switch to Recordings. Then look at the same call as Anna, who didn't record it.

You are
  1. The transcript follows the recording, as round 1 of the Home prototype drew it. A player docks under the transcript, any line plays from where it was said, and the line being played gets an outline. (It plays 3× here.)
  2. Recordings lists the file with its length, what it holds and its size, and Show in Finder. Clicking it opens the file in QuickTime Player, so we don't build a video player. The player under the transcript plays its sound.
  3. Only the person who recorded has the file for now. Everyone else gets the transcript (the backend sends it to them), and their Recordings tab says who has the recording.
  4. The gear next to your name opens Settings.
